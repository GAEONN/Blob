# Blob Dashboard

Mode: Operate. A separate native companion, not a replacement for SmallBlob.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The user's CarPlay-like overview exposes Music, System, Sound and Gaming together.
No sequential tab switching for everyday information.

OWN-WORLD: Reuse Blob's actual live GPU glass, adaptive ink, filled transport and continuous
corners. Preserve readable text and concentric radii: outer = inner + inset.

STORY: Read hardware while playing music, adjust sound without leaving the overview, then
detach Gaming into a narrow, click-through dock. Settings are contextual, not a new page.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full work-area window. A slender Gaming instrument rail at left, a large
square-art Music region in the middle, and System above Sound on the right. Close, window
size and Settings sit in the header. No fabricated telemetry or album art in production.

FORM: Precise extension of the existing native interface, user-pinned car-display topology;
no replacement identity or concept tournament. Code-led, using incumbent GPU captures as
the craft reference. Signature motion: the 92-DIP Gaming bubble extends downward into a
left dock, with fixed top/left position and a restrained spring. SmallBlob source and
configuration are read-only throughout this task.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
